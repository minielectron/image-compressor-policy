Privacy Policy — ImageCompress Pro

Last updated: 6 July 2026

The short version: the app collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing about you. Every image is processed entirely on your device.

1. Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting service. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or usage data. The app does not require an internet connection to perform any of its functions.

2. Your photos

  • Selection. Images are chosen through the Android system photo picker or the device camera app. The app cannot browse your photo library — it only sees the specific image you choose.
  • Processing. Compression, cropping, and resizing run locally using Android platform codecs. No image is ever uploaded to any server.
  • Temporary files. Results are written to the app's private cache so you can preview, save, or share them. They are not accessible to other apps and are deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Saving. Tapping Save writes the image to your Pictures folder via Android MediaStore. The file stays on your device.
  • Sharing. Tapping Share hands the file to the app you pick in Android's share sheet, which is then governed by that app's privacy policy.

3. Face detection

For photo document presets (passport, exam photo), the app uses Google ML Kit's on-device face detection to position the automatic crop around the subject's face.

  • Detection runs entirely on your device. No image or face data is sent to Google or to us.
  • The app detects only the location and size of a face. It performs no face recognition, identification, or matching.
  • No face data or biometric template is stored — it exists in memory only while the crop is calculated.

If Google Play services is unavailable, the app falls back to a centered crop.

4. Image metadata (EXIF)

Photos often carry EXIF metadata such as camera model, date, and GPS location. The app offers an option (on by default) to remove this metadata from the compressed output. We never read it for our own purposes and never transmit it.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — not declared. Photos are taken by your device's own camera app through a system intent; the app has no direct camera access.
  • Photos — not declared. The app uses the system photo picker and does not request READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE.
  • Storage write — Android 9 and older only. On Android 9 (API 28) and below, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE is requested solely to save a compressed image to Pictures when you tap Save. It is never used to read your files. On Android 10+ it is not requested at all.

6. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Third-party services

The app bundles Google ML Kit face detection, which runs locally through Google Play services, used only as described in section 3. See Google's privacy policy for information about Google Play services itself. The app contains no advertising networks and no analytics providers.

8. Data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything stored locally, uninstall the app or clear its storage from Android Settings → Apps → ImageCompress Pro → Storage.
